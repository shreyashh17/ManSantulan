Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our AI assessment model, data privacy, standardized screenings, and crisis protocols.

How does the AI balance calculator compute my score?

Our backend executes an ensemble Random Forest regression model trained on 5,000 real student lifestyle surveys. It assesses non-linear interactions between your daily screen time, phone unlocks, sleep duration, physical activity, and academic stress level to output an equilibrium index between 0.0 and 10.0.

Is my assessment data private and secure?

Yes. ManSantulan adheres to zero-knowledge data minimization principles. Your input values are processed securely in memory. You can take the assessment anonymously as a guest without creating an account. If registered, you can download your complete data record or permanently delete it at any time via the Privacy Center.

Does this replace a psychologist or clinical diagnosis?

No. ManSantulan is strictly an awareness, psycho-education, and lifestyle reflection platform. It does not diagnose clinical depression, anxiety disorders, or ADHD. If your score reflects persistent distress, we encourage consulting a licensed mental health professional or calling Tele-MANAS (14416).

Are the ambient soundscapes recorded audio files or synthesized?

All ambient soundscapes (gentle rain, ocean waves, forest breeze, pink noise) are procedurally generated in real time using the browser's native Web Audio API. This means zero third-party audio downloads, zero tracking cookies, and instant offline functionality.

Which languages are supported?

ManSantulan currently provides fluent English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Marathi (मराठी) localization via our client-side translation dictionary. You can toggle your preferred language at any time in the navigation bar.

Educational Notice: ManSantulan is strictly an awareness and self-reflection platform. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace licensed clinical psychiatric evaluation.
