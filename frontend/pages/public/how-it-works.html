🔬 The 3-Step Methodology

From Daily Habits to Actionable Peace

How ManSantulan converts honest self-reflection into non-clinical, scientifically sound mental health equilibrium.

01

1. Answer 12 Mindful Questions

Our streamlined 4-step wizard guides you through your daily digital routine: daily screen hours, estimated phone unlocks, primary platform, purpose of use (entertainment vs. education), sleep duration, physical activity, and perceived exam stress.

No Invasive Permissions Takes ~2 Minutes Preset Quick-Fills
02

2. Random Forest Regression Analysis

The backend pipeline applies log-transforms and standard scaling to your study and sleep metrics before passing them through our pre-trained Random Forest model. Your responses are benchmarked against 5,000 real student lifestyle profiles to compute an equilibrium score between 0.0 and 10.0.

Scikit-Learn Pipeline Low Latency (<50ms) Trained on 5,000 Records
03

3. Actionable Coping & Wellness Pathways

Instead of a scary medical label, you receive an intuitive SVG ring gauge, clear explanations of your top contributing habits, and 3 personalized micro-steps. High-stress profiles are gently guided to Tele-MANAS (14416) and student counseling resources.
