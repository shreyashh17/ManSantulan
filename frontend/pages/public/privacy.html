🔒 Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Privacy Policy & Data Ethics

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective for all Indian and Global Students

1. Our Fundamental Commitment

At ManSantulan (मन संतुलन), student privacy is not a checkbox; it is our foundation. We believe that honest mental health reflection can only happen in a safe, unmonitored environment. We never sell student data, we never monetize personal habits, and we never share your scores with universities, parents, or advertisers.

2. Data We Collect and Why

When you interact with the platform, we collect only the minimum information necessary:

  • Assessment Habits: 12 lifestyle inputs (age, gender, country, academic level, primary platform, purpose, usage hours, unlocks, study hours, physical activity, sleep, stress rank).
  • Account Credentials: If registered, your name, student email, and Bcrypt cryptographic password hash. We never store passwords in plaintext.
  • Self-Care Reflections: Private journal entries and mood check-ins. These are accessible only to your authenticated session.

3. Guest Anonymous Use

You are welcome to use our AI predictor and wellness tools entirely without an account. In guest mode, calculations occur transiently and are not tied to any identifiable profile.

4. Your Data Portability Rights

Under our Privacy Center (visit /privacy-center), you have total ownership:

  • Instant Export: Download all your historical scores and settings as an open JSON file.
  • Instant Deletion: Permanently wipe your account, audit logs, and assessment records with a single click.
Educational Notice: ManSantulan is strictly an awareness and self-reflection platform. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace licensed clinical psychiatric evaluation.
