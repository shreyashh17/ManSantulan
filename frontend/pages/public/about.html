🌿 Our Mission

Balance of Mind for Every Student

"ManSantulan" (मन संतुलन) bridges empirical data science and psychological warmth, helping Indian university students understand their digital rhythms without judgment or stigma.

🛡️

100% Non-Clinical & Safe

We replace scary diagnostic verdicts with compassionate, constructive self-awareness. Stressed students find clarity within 5 seconds.

📊

Grounded in Real Data

Trained on 5,000 real student lifestyle records, correlating screen exposure, sleep architecture, and study habits with wellbeing outcomes.

🇮🇳

Culturally Contextualized

Tailored to Indian academic realities — exam crunch seasons, late-night hostel schedules, and direct integration with Tele-MANAS (14416).

The Story Behind ManSantulan

As students ourselves, we noticed how late-night social media scrolling combined with intense academic expectations created silent burnout cycles. Existing mental health tools were either clinical, alarming, or Western-centric. ManSantulan was created to provide a soothing sanctuary: an evidence-based balance index, gentle daily micro-practices, and immediate access to accredited counselors.
