Evidence-Based Student Wellness Guides

🧠

Deconstructing Academic Burnout

Recognizing the subtle shift from productive focus to emotional exhaustion, brain fog, and chronic cynicism towards coursework.

  • Set explicit daily "off-clock" hours where schoolwork is paused.
  • Replace perfectionism with iterative progress ("good enough" submission).
📱

Social Media & Comparative Anxiety

How curated highlight reels of peer accomplishments trigger subconscious feelings of inadequacy and imposter syndrome.

  • Conduct a monthly unfollow or mute audit of triggering accounts.
  • Remember that public posts capture outcomes, never the struggle behind them.
🌙

Sleep Architecture for Exam Stamina

Why all-nighters deplete hippocampal memory consolidation and increase exam errors by up to 30%.

  • Prioritize 7 hours of sleep over last-minute cramming before testing.
  • Maintain dark, cool sleeping quarters free from vibrating phones.
🌿

Somatic Grounding for Test Panic

Simple 5-4-3-2-1 sensory exercises to anchor racing thoughts right before or during difficult examinations.

  • Notice 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear.
  • Take 3 deep diaphragmatic breaths through your nose into the abdomen.
